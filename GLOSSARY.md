# Belote

An online version of classic belote ("à la retourne") for four people playing live in teams of two.

## People

**User**:
A registered person, known to other Users by a unique username, who signs in with their email and password.
_Avoid_: Account, member

**Player**:
A User seated in a particular Game.
_Avoid_: Participant

**Seat**:
One of the four places at a Game's table, in clockwise order. Each is occupied by one Player.
_Avoid_: Position, place

**Team**:
The two Players in opposite Seats of a Game, who score together.
_Avoid_: Partnership, side

## Getting a game going

**Lobby**:
The shared waiting place Users join. Every time four Users are waiting, they leave it together, seated in a new Game.
_Avoid_: Queue, waiting room, table

## Play

**Game**:
The whole match, from four Players being seated until one Team wins.
_Avoid_: Match, partie, table

**Deal**:
One cycle of shuffling, dealing, choosing trump, playing eight Tricks and scoring.
_Avoid_: Hand, round, donne, manche

**Trick**:
The four cards played in turn, one by each Player, and won by one of them.
_Avoid_: Pli, round

## Choosing Trump

**Dealer**:
The Player who deals the cards for a Deal.

**Turned card**:
The card shown face up after each Player has received their first five cards, offering its suit as Trump.
_Avoid_: Retourne, up-card

**Trump round**:
One pass around the table in which each Player in turn either passes or takes. A Deal has one or two.
_Avoid_: Bidding round, tour

**Trump**:
The suit chosen for a Deal, whose cards beat every card of the other suits.
_Avoid_: Atout

**Taker**:
The Player who chose Trump for a Deal.
_Avoid_: Preneur, bidder

**Taking Team**:
The Taker's Team for that Deal.
_Avoid_: Attackers

**Defending Team**:
The Team opposing the Taking Team for that Deal.
_Avoid_: Defenders' side

## Scoring

**Contract**:
The Taking Team's obligation to finish the Deal ahead of the Defending Team.

**Dedans**:
The Taking Team failing its Contract.
_Avoid_: Chute, going set

**Capot**:
One Team winning all eight Tricks of a Deal.
_Avoid_: Slam, vole

**Dix de der**:
The bonus for winning the last Trick of a Deal.
_Avoid_: Last-trick bonus

**Belote**:
One Player holding both the King and the Queen of Trump in a Deal. "Rebelote" is the call made when the second of the two is played.

**Card points**:
The value of the cards a Team has won in its Tricks during a Deal.

**Deal points**:
A Team's Card points plus its Dix de der and Belote for one Deal; the two Teams' Deal points are compared to settle the Contract.
_Avoid_: Total, trick points

**Score**:
A Team's running total over the Game.
